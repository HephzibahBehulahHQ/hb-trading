## About HB Trading

**HB Trading** is the financial markets, trading analytics and research division of **Hephzibah Behulah**.

We develop digital tools, educational resources and analytical platforms designed to help individuals understand financial markets, evaluate trading strategies, manage risks and make informed financial decisions.

Our mission is to make financial analysis more accessible, transparent and data-driven through practical technology.

## Our Products and Development Areas

### 1. Trading Dashboard

A centralized platform for monitoring market information, analyzing trading activity and visualizing financial data.

### 2. Trading Journal

A digital journal for recording trades, reviewing performance, tracking mistakes and improving trading discipline.

### 3. Portfolio Analytics

Tools for tracking investments, visualizing portfolio performance and understanding asset allocation.

### 4. Risk Management Calculator

Applications for position sizing, risk-to-reward calculations, capital allocation and potential loss assessment.

### 5. Strategy Backtesting

Tools for evaluating trading strategies using historical market data and clearly defined assumptions.

### 6. Financial Education

Beginner-friendly educational resources covering financial markets, trading fundamentals, technical analysis and risk management.

*These are planned product areas. Availability and development status will be documented individually.*

## Architecture Rules

1. Keep the brand website separate from individual trading applications.
2. Maintain independent product repositories and deployments.
3. Validate financial calculations and data transformations.
4. Clearly identify historical, simulated and live data.
5. Never represent hypothetical performance as guaranteed returns.
6. Protect sensitive financial and personal information.
7. Follow applicable financial, privacy and consumer protection requirements.
8. Use approved corporate design standards.
